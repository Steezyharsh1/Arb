# Fee readiness and independent-pair audit

**As of:** 2026-10-10 (UTC)
**Scope:** the three venues enabled in `config/venues.yaml` — Kalshi, Polymarket US, and ForecastEx — plus the IBKR/ForecastEx route record. Official documentation was opened directly; this is a documentation review, **not** a live API, account-statement, or fee-reconciliation test. No account credentials or account evidence were available.

## Executive result

- **Three unordered venue pairs are provenance-independent:** Kalshi–Polymarket US, Kalshi–ForecastEx, and Polymarket US–ForecastEx. This means the registered DCM/issuer/matching/settlement identities differ; it does **not** mean the pairs have complete costs or usable books.
- **Zero of the three pairs is presently end-to-end evaluable by the ordinary paper pipeline.** Only Kalshi currently reports `APIStatus.VERIFIED`. Polymarket US is `API_DOCUMENTED_UNTESTED`; ForecastEx is a structured adapter stub. Collect and paper discovery admit only `VERIFIED` APIs. Also, with no configured LLM, matching emits no `APPROVED` certificate, and `search_event()` requires one.
- **No active venue has a complete all-in fee schedule in this checkout.** Kalshi trade and settlement are incomplete; Polymarket US trade and settlement are incomplete; ForecastEx trade and settlement are incomplete. The former Polymarket US coefficient (`0.06`) is stale, and the prior ForecastEx-wide “complete / zero settlement” claim exceeded the evidence.
- `search_event()` can calculate a synthetic, pre-approved pair if a caller supplies two contracts and two books. That API-level capability is not an end-to-end venue result. Even with books, all three actual pairs hit at least one mandatory `FEE_DATA_INCOMPLETE` component, so net profit stays unavailable rather than estimated.

- The standard Polymarket 99-row, 100-contract table, half-even examples, per-fill maker rebate, and aggressive-order cap are arithmetic regressions against the **published standard reference values**. They use a **test-only synthetic schedule with only the trade calculation marked complete**; production trade and settlement statuses remain incomplete.
- A separate synthetic fixed-fee regression checks the documented ForecastEx `$0.01` exchange component per executed contract. It does not test an IBKR account, all-in commissions, incentives, or settlement.
- Production `FeeCalculator` records keep each current active schedule `INCOMPLETE`; production trade/settlement calls must raise `FEE_DATA_INCOMPLETE`. Kalshi's fee page has no global effective date, so its config stores only the 2026-10-10 reference-snapshot date; an absent effective date independently blocks fee use. `evaluate_two_leg()` therefore leaves `net_profit`, total mandatory costs, and capital unavailable (`None`/`n/a`), not zero and not an estimate.
- No current live API payload, account route, customer fee report, settlement statement, or actual order/fill reconciliation was available. All three active adapters therefore remain as they were: only Kalshi's public API status is verified; the other two cannot form a paper pair.

## Exact operator actions to close each gap

Use an authorized account and public read-only endpoints where possible. **Do not paste credentials, private keys, account IDs, or unredacted statements into chat or Git.** Save redacted evidence under the existing ignored `reports/evidence/` convention, with UTC capture time, market/event/series identifier, route, and source URL.

1. **Kalshi — market fee context:** for every candidate market, capture its `series_ticker` and `event_ticker`; call `GET https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/series/{series_ticker}`, `GET https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events/{event_ticker}`, `GET /series/fee_changes?series_ticker=...`, and paginated `GET /events/fee_changes?event_ticker=...`. Preserve effective timestamps and null/cleared overrides. Do not put signed headers or credentials in evidence files.
2. **Kalshi — account/fill rounding:** have the account owner confirm direct-member versus non-direct route and `$0.0001` versus `$0.01` balance precision from the authorized account record. Export an existing multi-fill order's order ID, fill prices/quantities and timestamps, actual fee/balance adjustments, and final order fee; a permitted demo/paper drill may supplement but not replace production-account evidence. Reconcile the documented six-decimal and per-order accumulator behavior, including maker/taker fills, before implementing a cost model. If order partition/account precision is unavailable, leave trade fees blocked.
3. **Kalshi — settlement:** obtain a representative simple yes/no statement and, if scalar/sub-cent contracts are in scope, a scalar settlement/fee example from the current account or an accessible authoritative rule clause. Add an explicit contract-type guard before representing any zero fee. If scalar evidence is unavailable, reject that contract type rather than generalizing binary treatment.
4. **Polymarket US — market fee family:** run the existing read-only `python scripts/polymarket_us_smoke.py` from a network-reachable machine and capture redacted raw market payloads for a standard market and Table Tennis. Verify the actual payload-to-fee-family mapping against the current fee documentation; do not infer Table Tennis from generic `sports`. For combos, capture the authenticated combo instrument response and either implement its fee curve or explicitly reject/exclude combo instruments. Provide code/tests for every supported family before changing production trade status.
5. **Polymarket US — actual fees and settlement:** from an authorized account, export fee and execution reports for an existing multi-fill order under the applicable fee family (for the current schedule, executed after 2026-10-07 10:00 ET) and for a settled position. The reporting API provides fee reports/history, with downloadable ledger history available from 2026-05-01. Reconcile trade/order IDs, fee amounts, rounding/cap adjustments, and net settlement cash movements. Ask the venue for the customer settlement-fee rule if the report alone is ambiguous; do not infer zero from a missing ledger line, especially where suppressed `LEDGER_ENTRY_TYPE_BALANCE_SETTLEMENT_FEE` entries are filtered out. Do not use the account's prior-month tier as a per-order discount. If no suitable existing execution is available, do not create a live order; retain the block.
6. **ForecastEx/IBKR — all-in trade route:** identify whether the account is direct IBKR client, introduced/advisor, or another FCM route. Obtain the current account-specific commission schedule and a redacted trade confirmation/statement proving both the broker/FCM charge and the `$0.01` exchange component per executed side. Confirm whether any monthly coupon/member incentive is actually earned and passed through. Until route and statement agree, keep the total blocked even though the exchange component is published.
7. **ForecastEx/IBKR — settlement:** obtain the complete current Rule 603 settlement-fee text directly from the CFTC/ForecastEx/FCM source or a written venue/broker response, plus a settled-account example. Confirm Rules 416/417 and any member incentive terms if they can affect the customer's net cost. The currently accessible extraction is insufficient. Do not promote settlement to zero based only on the transaction-fee filing or FAQ payout description.

No step requires a new live order. Prefer existing statements or an authorized demo/paper environment; do not create an unapproved position to satisfy this review.

## System-level gates and release criteria

- `strict_fee_freshness` defaults to `False` in `evaluate_two_leg()` and `run_arbitrage_scan()`; current paper/historical/backtest callers do not override it. Live mode is monitor-only and does not scan for execution. Freshness dates in this ledger are provenance, **not an enforced expiry gate today**. Before any schedule is promoted for a paper/live path, wire freshness and effective-date selection explicitly (historical replay also needs a fee schedule valid at the recorded trade time).
- No account-specific commission, rebate, fee family, fill state, or settlement treatment is allowed to become an execution-facing estimate. Missing mandatory cost remains `None` and blocks.
- A production schedule may be promoted only after the evidence above is stored, the selected account route is explicit, the implementation covers each relevant fee family and state, regression tests exercise the real published behavior, and an authorized statement reconciles the calculation. `live_test_status` stays `UNAVAILABLE` until a real venue/account test is actually performed.
- After each evidence update, update `config/fees.yaml`, this page, the matching venue-research row, and the handoff/roadmap in the **same focused change**. Keep the three concepts separate: provenance independence, API reachability/status, and all-in fee readiness.
